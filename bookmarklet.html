Live Editor — edit on the page itself

Not a separate tool page. Not a box with the site inside. This works like VisBug and Edit Page: you open your live site, tap the bookmark, and edit that page right there, full screen.

✏️ Edit This Site

Drag the button above into your bookmarks bar. That is the whole install.

How to use it

  1. Drag ✏️ Edit This Site into your bookmarks bar (desktop Chrome).
  2. Open any of your live sites, for example https://dineshmerugu06.github.io/footwear-v2/
  3. Click the Edit This Site bookmark. A black bar appears at the top of the page.
  4. Click any element on the page to select it. Change its text, colour, background, size, drag it to move it, hide it, delete it.
  5. Every change goes in the Change Log and saves to GitHub by itself. The live site updates in about 1 minute.

On your phone there is no bookmarks bar: open the site, open Chrome's bookmarks menu, tap Edit This Site.

Saving

First time you press Save, it asks for a GitHub token. Make one at https://github.com/settings/tokens — fine-grained, Contents Read and write, only the repo you are editing. It stays in your browser only, never in chat.

No token? Press Change Log, then Copy log, and send the log to Muse. He saves it from the backend.
